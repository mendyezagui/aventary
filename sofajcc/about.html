🛋 About The Sofa: Your Jewish Community Home

The Sofa Jewish Community Center (Chabad) is dedicated to providing a comfortable, welcoming, and inspiring spiritual hub for every Jew, regardless of background or affiliation. Our unique name, “The Sofa,” embodies our mission: to create a relaxed, home-like environment where you can truly be yourself, ask questions, and connect to your Jewish heritage in a meaningful way.

Our Vision

Inspired by the teachings of the Lubavitcher Rebbe, Rabbi Menachem M. Schneerson, our vision is to foster a vibrant and knowledgeable Jewish community through unconditional love (Ahavat Yisrael), outreach, and education. We believe that every Jew is a letter in the same Torah scroll, and together, we complete the picture.
