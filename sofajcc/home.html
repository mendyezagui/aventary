WELCOME HOME

More than a place of prayer — a spiritual home

We are a warm and welcoming Chabad Jewish Community Center dedicated to fostering a deep connection to Jewish life and tradition.

Our shul is more than just a place of prayer; it is a spiritual home where families, friends, and neighbors gather for lively prayer services, engaging Torah study, meaningful holidays, and heartfelt community connection.

Inspired by the Rebbe's vision, we embrace every individual with unconditional love and strive to ignite the soul of Judaism in a comfortable, family-focused atmosphere. Come sit, learn, and grow with us.
